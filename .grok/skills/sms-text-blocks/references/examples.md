# SMS rewrite examples

Use these as the rhythm target. Copy the blank-line pattern, not the topics.

## Example A — cut a research dump

Source (do not send)
Long paragraph on barrel sauna dimensions, ship date, circuit, comparison to steam shower, three product options, and a closing recommendation.

SMS block

🪦 Redwood Vista 6-person barrel

71 L x 73 W
full glass back wall
door opposite

📦 Ship listed ~Oct 22
if ordered mid-Sep

⚡ 240V 40A circuit
not a steam shower swap

❓ Want me to order?
